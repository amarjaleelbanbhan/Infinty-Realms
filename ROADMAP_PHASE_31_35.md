# Infinity Realms - Endgame & Deployment Roadmap (Phases 31-35)

This roadmap brings the game to full market readiness, focusing on highly advanced "multi-AI agent" systems, extreme polish, monetization, and production-grade cloud deployment.

## Phase 31: Multi-Agent AI Expansion
- **Simulated AI Players**: Complex NPCs that roam the world, accept quests, level up, and invite real players to parties.
- **AI Dynamic Negotiation**: Merchants that remember your past trades and dynamically haggle prices via the LLM.
- **AI Crime & Guard System**: Guards that run a reasoning loop to investigate crimes (stealing, player killing) and hold dynamic trials.

## Phase 32: Visual Polish & Quality of Life
- **Cinematic Combat Polish**: Screen shake, advanced particle emitters for spells, and critical hit zoom-ins.
- **Immersive Soundscapes**: Dynamic ambient tracks that crossfade based on time-of-day and biome.
- **Settings Menu**: Comprehensive UI for adjusting volume, graphics, and keybinds.

## Phase 33: Monetization & Retention
- **Premium Cosmetic Shop**: Fully validated backend for purchasing skins, pets, and mounts without pay-to-win.
- **Daily Rewards & Streaks**: A retention system that rewards players for logging in consecutively.
- **Leaderboard Tiers**: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond brackets for seasonal rewards.

## Phase 34: Cloud Infrastructure & Scaling
- **Kubernetes Auto-Scaling**: Helm charts and K8s manifests for scaling the game servers dynamically based on player count.
- **NGINX Reverse Proxy**: Load balancing across multiple NodeJS server instances.
- **Redis Pub/Sub Expansion**: Multi-server cross-chat and global trade channels.

## Phase 35: Marketing Tools & Launch Readiness
- **Spectator Camera AI**: An autonomous camera that flies around the map recording epic battles for marketing.
- **Chunk Loading Optimization**: Advanced Phaser optimizations to ensure 60fps on mobile and low-end hardware.
- **Final Code Audit**: Security audit, type checking, and test coverage guarantees.
