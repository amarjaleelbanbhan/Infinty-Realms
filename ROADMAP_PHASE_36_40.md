# Infinity Realms - LiveOps & Ecosystem Expansion (Phases 36-40)

Now that the game is market-ready, these phases focus on LiveOps, post-launch expansions, deep lore generation, and community-driven ecosystems.

## Phase 36: Cross-Platform & Accessibility
- **Gamepad Support**: Map all UI and combat interactions to standard controllers (Xbox/PlayStation).
- **Mobile Polish**: Enhanced touch joysticks and responsive CSS scaling for all modal windows.

## Phase 37: Optional Web3 Integration
- **Cosmetic NFT Minting**: Allow players to optionally mint their earned cosmetics to the blockchain.
- **Decentralized Trade**: Optional integration for trading items as tokens.

## Phase 38: LiveOps & Seasonal Content
- **Dynamic AI Seasons**: AI determines seasonal events (e.g., "The Long Winter" brings snow biomes everywhere).
- **Event Pass (Battle Pass)**: An XP track offering limited-time seasonal rewards.

## Phase 39: Deep Lore & Procedural History
- **Procedural Books**: Scattered throughout the world, players can find books whose contents are generated by the LLM based on the world's seed.
- **Lore UI**: A compendium tab to read discovered history.

## Phase 40: Creator Economy & Modding API
- **User-Generated Dungeons**: Players can design custom dungeon layouts.
- **AI Moderation**: The AI Dungeon Master reviews and approves user-generated content for fairness and appropriateness before publishing to the global realm.
