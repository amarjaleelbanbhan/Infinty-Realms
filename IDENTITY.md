# Infinity Realms — Game Identity & Uniqueness Proposal

*Every session is different. Every decision changes your story.*

---

## 🔮 The Core Concept: The Leyline Seeker

Unlike typical fantasy games that rely on standard classes (Warrior, Mage, Rogue) copied from classic RPGs, *Infinity Realms* centers entirely on the concept of the **Leyline Seeker**.

Your character is a traveler bound to the planet's pulse. In this world, magic is not an internal pool of mana; it is a current that flows through the earth along lines of power (Leylines). 

### 1. Biome-Reactive Magic Grimoire
Your spell casting behaves differently based on where you stand:
- **Plains**: Spells deal wind and kinetic damage, pushing enemies back.
- **Forest**: Spells entangle enemies, leeching their life force to heal you.
- **Desert**: Spells conjure quicksand traps and blinding dust storms.
- **Volcano**: Spells manifest explosive lava pools.
- **Snow**: Spells freeze enemies in place.
- **Swamp**: Spells inflict toxic poisons.

This makes positioning and travel highly strategic. You are not just fighting monsters; you are negotiating with the environment itself.

## ⚙️ Original Game Mechanics ("I've never seen this before")

To stand out in the crowded RPG space, *Infinity Realms* relies on three interconnected loops that combine elements of sandbox simulation, automation, and roguelikes:

### 1. Leyline Harvesting & Automation Networks (Factorio-inspired)
Instead of just gathering gold from drops, players place **Leyline Nodes** (Essence Nodes, Mana Relays, Arcane Forges) across biomes.
- These nodes tap into the planet's veins to passively collect essence.
- In later phases, these link together via magical relay paths to feed energy back to player-owned **Guild Halls** and **Bazaar Stalls**.
- Players must defend their automation loops from regional monster raids triggered by high leyline stress.

### 2. Ecosystem Cascade (RimWorld/Dwarf Fortress-inspired)
The world behaves as a living ecosystem:
- Overhunting a specific creature (e.g., wolves) will cause prey animals (e.g., rabbits) to multiply unchecked, leading to crop destruction in neighboring cities.
- Crop failures lead to destitution, forcing city NPCs to turn into bandits on the roads or travel to other cities as refugees.
- Players' quests are dynamically shaped by this state: an AI Dungeon Master event might ask the player to cull rabbits, hunt rogue bandits, or establish trade routes to save a starving town.

### 3. AI Dungeon Master Catalyst
Dynamic crises are triggered in real-time based on world ages, seasons, and player achievements:
- **Seasonal Shifts** every 30 days trigger cataclysms (e.g., *Blood Moon Tides*, *Volcanic Winter*).
- Weather effects (e.g., rain, snow, auroras) alter combat dynamics, spell costs, and movement speeds.

## 🎨 Aesthetics & Color Palette

The visual identity of *Infinity Realms* relies on a **Mystic Abyssal Void** aesthetic. We avoid typical generic fantasy colors (bright reds, flat blues) in favor of high-contrast, gem-like hues set against deep dark night backdrops:

- **Background (Abyssal Void)**: `#07071a`
- **Surface (Deep Night)**: `#0f0f2a`
- **Leyline Violet (Accent)**: `#7c6bff`
- **Warm Amber Gold (Currency & Reward)**: `#ffcb47`
- **Leyline Green (XP & Life)**: `#3fffa0`
- **Astral Blue (Mana)**: `#4d9fff`

This creates a high-end, neon-fantasy glow that gives the game a futuristic yet ancient aesthetic.
